## 📱 PC ane Mobile banne ma
**Mobile ma fakt JOVANU** (📅 Aaj, 🗓️ Calendar, ⏳ Queue, 📇 Leads, 📊 Analytics, 📜 Kaam) — badli, post, approve ke delete mobile thi nahi thay; badhu kaam PC par.
(Mobile thi pan approve karvu hoy to config.json ma `"mobile_mode": "full"`.)

1. 🔗 Jodo → **"Mobile/server PIN"** (APP_PIN) ma 8+ akshar no PIN rakho (dakhla `Kheti@2026`) → Save
2. `App_PC_ane_Mobile.bat` chalavo (Mac/Linux: `./app_pc_ane_mobile.sh`)
3. Kaali window ma **"Mobile par (same WiFi): https://192.168.x.x:8443"** aave — e link mobile na Chrome ma kholo
4. Pehli vaar chetavani aave → "Advanced → aagal vadho" (aa tamaru potanu taalu chhe) → PIN nakho
5. Chrome menu → **"Add to Home screen"** — have mobile par app nu icon
- Mobile **ghar na e j WiFi** par hovo joie, ane PC chalu. Bahar thi nahi khule (surakshit).
- Mobile thi 30 min kai na karo to jate bahar; 5 vaar khoto PIN → 15 min band.
- Fakt PC ma vaparvu hoy to juno `App_Chalu_Karo.bat` j chalavo.
